# Landmarks

- STATUS: OPEN
- PRIORITY: 75
- TAGS: worldgen structures exploration

Add rare, memorable procedural points of interest (POIs) across the world outside of standard towns to guide visual navigation on the horizon:
- **Ancient Ruins & Stonehenge Monoliths**: Weathered stone arches, obelisks, and mossy temple pillars on hilltops.
- **Lighthouses & Coastal Docks**: Tall beacons placed on rocky coastal promontories and island tips with warm night lamps.
- **Hilltop Castles & Wizard Towers**: Fortified keeps or solitary spires on elevated peaks with connecting mountain trails.
- **Giant Statues / World Trees**: Rare macro-cell centerpiece structures that flatten a small local plateau and suppress overlapping props.
