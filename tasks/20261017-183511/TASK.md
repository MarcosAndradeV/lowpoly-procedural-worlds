# More Biomes

- STATUS: OPEN
- PRIORITY: 70
- TAGS: worldgen biomes props

Expand the temperature, moisture, and continentalness classification in `src/biomes.rs` and `src/props.rs` with new distinct low-poly biomes and flora:
- **Swamp / Marshland**: Low-elevation high-moisture wetlands with murky water patches, willow/mangrove trees, and reeds.
- **Savanna / Badlands**: Warm semi-arid grasslands with flat-topped acacia trees and red-ochre strata.
- **Taiga / Boreal & Autumn Forest**: Cold pine/birch snow-transition forests and seasonal amber/crimson foliage clusters.
- **Crystal / Mushroom Caves or Oasis**: Rare sub-biome pockets to reward exploration.
