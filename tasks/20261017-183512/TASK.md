# Landmarks

- STATUS: CLOSED
- PRIORITY: 75
- TAGS: worldgen structures exploration

Add rare, memorable procedural points of interest (POIs) across the world outside of standard towns to guide visual navigation on the horizon:
- **Ancient Ruins & Stonehenge Monoliths**: Weathered stone arches, obelisks, central runic altar, and mossy monoliths on elevated knolls.
- **Lighthouses & Coastal Docks**: Tall red-and-white banded beacons on coastal promontories and island tips with warm night lamps, railings, and wooden fishing docks.
- **Hilltop Castles & Wizard Towers**: Solitary high-altitude spires on mountain peaks and volcanic crags with observation balconies and levitating arcane crystal orbs.
- **Colossal World Trees**: Giant centerpiece ancient trees with arching buttress roots, colossal gnarled trunks, and multi-tiered foliage canopies.
- **Ancient Sandstone Temples**: Stepped ziggurat pyramids with columned colonnades and golden relic altars in canyonlands.
- **Plateau Flattening & Prop Suppression**: Clean single-cell $O(1)$ plateau flattening with smoothstep transitions and complete suppression of overlapping trees/boulders.
- **Batched Rendering & Exploration HUD**: Horizon-wide batched GPU mesh rendering with frustum culling, distance-based discovery HUD banner, and hotkey `K` toggle.
